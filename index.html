About

It started with an obsession with numbers. I leaned straight into finance and ground my way through the CFA program, clearing Level I and Level II on my first attempts.

Today, I'm at Kroll (formerly Duff & Phelps) working as a portfolio valuation trainee. Most of my work lives in private equity: determining what private portfolio companies backed by major global PE funds are actually worth. That means tearing down illiquid assets, stress-testing cash flows, and deploying real-world frameworks—from Income (DCF) and Market approaches to Contingent Claim Analysis (OPM) and PWERM.

Outside of private equity, I spend my time tracking public markets, developing data-driven frameworks to analyze listed Indian businesses, and trading them when the opportunity aligns.

Simple philosophy: understand intrinsic value, respect market liquidity, protect capital, and automate everything tedious.
