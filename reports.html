1. Empirical Study of WACC for NSE-Listed Companies

Published: Mar 2026 • NSE India Market Universe
Downloads: [Download PDF Report] [Download Complete Excel Dataset & Model]

Overview

An empirical investigation into the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) across the full National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) universe. The study analyzes cross-sectional variation across 11 primary sectors, 50+ industries, and 16 synthetic credit rating categories. For every covered firm, three distinct WACC estimates were computed using Daily, Weekly, and Monthly beta estimation frequencies.

Core Macro Parameters: Risk-Free Rate (Rf): 6.67% • Equity Risk Premium (ERP): 5.23% • Market: National Stock Exchange (NSE) India.

Methodology

  • Universe Retrieval: Programmatically gathered all active NSE-listed tickers using Python (nselib).
  • Data Pipeline: Extracted historical adjusted price series and key balance sheet figures using yfinance.
  • Cost of Equity (Ke): Computed using the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM): Ke = Rf + β × ERP across three separate lookbacks:
    • Daily Beta (1-year horizon)
    • Weekly Beta (3-year horizon)
    • Monthly Beta (5-year horizon)
  • Cost of Debt (Kd): Derived using Aswath Damodaran's Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR) framework → Synthetic Credit Rating assignment → Default Spread mapping over the risk-free rate.
  • Capital Weights: Weighted based on prevailing market value of equity and book value of debt.
  • Data Processing: Cleaned and structured in Excel using dynamic array formulas (FILTER, INDEX, CHOOSECOLS).

Key Empirical Findings

  • Sector Dispersion: Real Estate exhibited the highest sector-wide average WACC at 13.67%, driven by elevated financial leverage and cyclicality. Healthcare recorded the lowest average WACC at 11.52% (a spread of ~215 bps).
  • Industry-Level Divergence: Industry-level variation was markedly wider than sector averages. Department Stores (16.9%) and Telecom Services (15.1%) had the highest discount rates, while Leisure (10.4%) and Electronic Gaming (8.7%) had the lowest.
  • Credit Rating Impact: Synthetic credit rating was identified as the single strongest driver of WACC differences. D/D2 rated distressed firms faced average WACCs of 16.96% compared to 11.70% for AAA/A1 rated companies (a spread exceeding 520 bps).
  • Beta Frequency Sensitivity: Choice of beta measurement interval materially altered individual discount rates (Daily Ke ≈ 12.4%, Weekly Ke ≈ 13.4%, Monthly Ke ≈ 12.6%).

Limitations & Future Scope

  • Employs synthetic credit ratings derived from interest coverage ratios rather than published agency credit ratings (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE).
  • Standard marginal corporate tax rate applied rather than firm-specific effective tax rates for after-tax cost of debt.
  • Future extensions will incorporate longitudinal tracking to evaluate WACC progression across changing interest rate regimes.

2. NTPC Limited — DCF Valuation Model & Investment Thesis

Completed: Feb 2026 • Ticker: NSE: NTPC
Downloads: [Download Presentation (PDF)] [Download Financial Model (Excel)]

Overview

A fundamental valuation and equity research project evaluating NTPC Limited, India's largest state-owned power generation utility. The study examines the company's dual mandate of sustaining regulated thermal return on equity while aggressively executing a transition to clean energy.

Model Structure & Analysis

  • 3-Statement Financial Modeling: Integrated income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement forecasts over a 5-year projection horizon.
  • Regulated Return Mechanism: Modeled Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) regulated 15.5% post-tax return on equity (ROE) framework for thermal assets.
  • Renewable Capacity Pipeline: Estimated capital outlay and return expectations for NTPC Green Energy subsidiary and solar/wind capacity additions.
  • Discounted Cash Flow (DCF): Unlevered Free Cash Flow (FCFF) discounted at a company-specific WACC with terminal value calculated using Gordon Growth and Exit Multiple approaches.
  • Scenario & Sensitivity Tables: Two-way sensitivity matrices analyzing implied share price sensitivity to changes in WACC (±100 bps) and terminal growth rate (±50 bps).
